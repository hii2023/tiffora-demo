Common questions

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. You share one link. It opens in the phone's browser and customers sign in with their mobile number and a 4 digit PIN.

Can I bring my existing customers?

Yes. Your team can add each customer with their plan and the meals they have left, so nobody starts again from zero.

Where does the customer's money go?

Straight into your own Razorpay account. Tiffora does not hold your money and takes no share of your sales. Razorpay's usual charges are between you and Razorpay.

I have many cash customers. Will it still work?

Yes. Your team can record cash, UPI or bank payments against a customer, and the pending amount updates by itself.

What if a customer wants to skip after the cut-off time?

They cannot, because you have already started cooking. You choose the cut-off time for each meal. Once it passes, that meal is locked and your count stays correct.

Is there a delivery boy app or WhatsApp messaging?

Not yet. Today you get a delivery list that you can download and share with your delivery staff. A delivery app and WhatsApp reminders are the next things we are building.
